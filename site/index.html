GradeCalc

A free grade calculator for your university courses. Works in your browser with no account needed. You'll be able to sign in with Google or Microsoft to sync across devices.

  • Grade so far & current averageWeighted components, dropped lowest, bonus marks, curves.
  • What do I need?The mark you need on what's left to hit your target, labelled as a projection.
  • Term & cumulative averages, GPACredit-weighted, with a choice of GPA scales.
  • GraphsYour grades over time, by term and by course.
  • Share course setupsSend classmates a course's structure (never your marks).
  • Excel, CSV & JSONExport everything, back it up, bring it back.
