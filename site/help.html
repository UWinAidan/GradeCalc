How to use GradeCalc

Everything here works without an account. Your marks stay in this browser unless you choose to sign in.

Getting started in 5 steps

  1. Open the calculator. Go to the calculator. The first time, you see three choices: Set up my first course, Try it with demo data (a made-up student to look around with) and Start with a term.
  2. Set up a course from your syllabus. Press Set up my first course (later: New course (set up) under Terms). Type the course Title, Code (optional) and Credits, then press Next.
  3. Copy the weights. Add each part of the grade from the syllabus (for example Assignments 20, Midterm 30, Final exam 50). The line under the list turns green at Weights = 100. Press Next and say how many items each part has (5 assignments, 1 midterm…), then any special rules, then a curve only if you already know it. Check the review and press Create course.
  4. Type marks as you get them. Click the dash next to an item and type the mark: 85 (percent), 17/20 (points) or a letter like B+. Press Enter. Or press + Mark (top bar on a computer, bottom-right button on a phone) to pick the course and item from a list.
  5. Read your results. Each course shows its Current average, the grade So far and a Projected grade. Type a target in the Need est. on … to finish with … % line to see what you need on what's left. Your term and cumulative averages and the Graphs update on their own. Download a backup now and then (More → Files).

Grades are calculated, not official. Check your school's own records for your real standing.

Worked example (demo data)

Press Try it with demo data (or Settings → Load demo data, which replaces what you have) and open Fall 2026 → DEMO-2300 Data and Chance. It's a made-up course in progress:

ComponentWeightMarks so far
Homework25%92, 88, —, — (4 items)
Tests35%74, — (2 tests)
Final exam40%— (not written yet)
  • Current average counts only what's marked. Homework averages 90% and Tests 74%, and those two parts are 60% of the course: (90 × 25 + 74 × 35) ÷ 60 = 80.67%, an A- on the UWindsor scale. This is "how am I doing on what's been graded".
  • So far counts every unmarked item as 0: Homework (92 + 88 + 0 + 0) ÷ 4 = 45% of 25, plus Tests (74 + 0) ÷ 2 = 37% of 35, plus 0 of 40 = 24.20%. This is the grade you'd get if you handed in nothing else. It only goes up.
  • What do I need? In the Need est. on … to finish with … % line, type 80. On everything left you need 79.7% on average on the 2 homeworks, the second test and the final: they're 70 points of weight (12.5 + 17.5 + 40), and (80 − 24.2) ÷ 70 = 79.7%. Pick Final exam instead of everything left and it says 79.0% on the final, if your other unmarked work goes like your current average (90% homework, 74% tests): that's 22.5 + 25.9 = 48.4 points, and (80 − 48.4) ÷ 40 = 79.0%.
  • Both "need" numbers and Projected are marked est.: they're projections, never saved as marks.

Every field explained

Most fields are in Edit structure (top bar; Edit on a phone). Press it again (Done editing) to go back to typing marks.

New course setup (the wizard)

New term name
Only when you have no term yet, or pick + New term…. "Fall 2026" style names fill the season and year for you.
Title · Code (optional) · Term · Credits
The course name, its code (any school's format, up to 20 characters), which term it's in, and its credits (a 6-credit course counts twice as much as a 3-credit one).
Copy the structure of another course (optional)
Starts from a course you already have: its components, weights, items, rules and curve, never its marks.
Components · bonus · + Component · ×
Name and weight of each part of the grade, as in the syllabus. Tick bonus for marks on top of 100. + Component adds a row; × removes one. The live line shows the total.
Items · Each out of · Drop lowest · Item weights
How many marks a component has (5 assignments), what each is out of (100 for percent, or 20 for "17/20"), how many of the lowest to drop, and Equal or Custom item weights (e.g. 1, 1, 2).
Final replaces the midterm when the final is higher · Final · Takes the weight of
Pick the final and the component(s) it replaces. The final takes their weight if your final mark is higher.
Minimum needed on a component · Component · Minimum %
Shows a warning when a component is below a minimum (for example labs ≥ 50%). It never changes the grade.
Excused items
For each component: Stay here, → another component, or By date (before / after a date). See Excused items go to below.
Course curve · Points (±) / Factor / Curved grade %
Only if you already know it: No curve, + points, × factor or Set grade %, then the number.
Review · Back · Next · Create course
The last page lists everything. Nothing is saved until you press Create course, and one Undo removes the whole setup.

Add a term · + Course

Name · Year of study (for year averages)
Start with a term or + Term opens Add a term. Name it like "Fall 2026". Year of study is optional (1–10).
Title · Code · Credits · Type
+ Course on a term (in Edit structure) adds a course by name only. Add its components later in Edit structure. Use New course (set up) to get the wizard instead.

Term

Name · Season · Year
Any name. Season (Fall, Winter, Spring, Summer) and year are filled in from names like "Winter 2027", or pick them yourself.
Year of study
Optional, 1–10. Used for the Years of study averages. A term without one counts only in the cumulative average.
Archived
Folds the term under "Archived terms" and + Mark skips its courses. It still counts in every average and graph.
New course (set up) · + Course · Delete term
Add a course with the wizard or by name only. Deleting a term deletes its courses too (restorable from Recently deleted for 30 days).

Term order is time order: it decides which attempt is the "latest" for repeats and the order on the graphs.

Course

Title · Code · Short name
Short name is optional and used where space is tight.
Credits
0–30. Averages are credit-weighted. Blank counts as 0.
Type
Graded (counts in averages), Pass/fail (credits shown separately, never in averages), Transfer credit (credits only) or Withdrawn (counts nowhere).
Course curve · Points (±) / Factor / Curved grade %
A curve on the whole course, applied after bonus and before the cap.
Cap at 100%
The course grade can't go over 100%, even with bonus or a curve.
Final grade (set)
The official grade once you have it, as a % or a letter (A+ to D-, or F). It replaces the calculated grade in every average. A letter alone counts as the middle of its range and is marked est. Clear the box to go back to the calculated grade.
Notes
Anything you want to remember (up to 2000 characters).
Move to term · Move
Moves the course, marks and all, to another term. Undo in the toast moves it back.
Repeat of an earlier attempt? · Which attempt counts · Not a repeat
Links a retake to the earlier attempt, then picks the default, Best attempt counts, Latest attempt counts or All attempts count. Not a repeat removes the link. The link only appears once you have another graded course to link to.
Duplicate course… · Save as template · Delete course
See FAQ and Files.
Mark complete · Reopen
On the course (not in Edit structure). Tags it Complete and takes it off the + Mark list. It doesn't change any numbers.
Pass · Fail
On a Pass/fail course: the result. Passed credits are shown next to your credit total.

Component (Assignments, Midterm, Final…)

Weight %
Its share of the course. The Weights = 100 line checks the total. Over 100 counts the extra as bonus. Under 100, So far and Projected are still out of 100 (the missing weight is never earned) while Current average is out of the weight marked. You'll see a warning; nothing is blocked.
Bonus (on top of 100)
Its marks add to the course grade on top of 100.
Drop lowest (so far) · Drop lowest (average)
How many of the lowest marks to drop, separately for the So far grade and the Current average (in "so far" an unmarked 0 would otherwise always be the one dropped).
Combine items
Mean (every item equal) or By item weight.
Projected % (est.)
What you expect on its unmarked items. Used for Projected and "what do I need" on other parts. Never saved as a mark.
Rule
Takes weight of… if higher (e.g. the final replaces the midterm when it's higher) or Minimum warning (with Minimum % and Warning label).
Excused items go to
Stay here (the other items share the weight), → another component, or By date (Date of a component, Before it →, On/after, or no date →).
Split into parts · Share of group · + Part · Delete part
Turns a component into a group (e.g. Labs = reports + quizzes). Parts share the group's weight by their share numbers: 2 and 1 = two thirds and one third. A group has its own Weight %, Bonus (on top of 100) and Warn below 50%.
+ Item · Bulk add / edit · Duplicate · Delete component
+ Item adds one item. Duplicate copies the component with its items, no marks, named "(copy)" (a "takes weight of" rule isn't copied). Bulk add / edit: see the FAQ.

Item (one mark)

Mark
85 or 85% (percent), 17/20 (points; sets "out of"), or a letter A+ to F (counts as the middle of the letter's range, marked est.). On an item out of 20, a plain 17 means 17 points. Blank = not marked yet. 0 is a real zero.
Item
The item's name (click it in the marks list to rename).
Out of
What the item is marked out of. 1 means a percent mark.
Item weight
Only with Combine items: By item weight. Blank = 1.
Counts in
Both, So far, Average or Neither.
Excused
The item doesn't count; its weight goes where the component says.
Curve
In the item's own points: +2, -1, ×1.1 or =45 (the adjusted mark). Empty = none.
Proj. %
Your expected mark on this item (a projection).
Date
Optional, in the marks list. Used by "excused by date" and the graphs.
×
Deletes the item. One with no mark goes at once (Undo in the toast); one with a mark asks first. Both go to Recently deleted.

+ Mark (add a mark)

Course · Item · Name
Pick the course and the item, or + New item in … (then give it a Name). Only graded courses that aren't complete or archived are listed.
Points / out of · % or letter
Type 17 / 20, or 85 or B+. A preview shows how the course, term and cumulative averages change. Press Save.

Results and projections

Current average
Only marked work counts. "How am I doing so far on what's been graded?"
So far
Unmarked work counts as 0. The floor: what you'd get if you stopped now.
Projected (est.)
Unmarked work at your projections (or your current average where you set none).
Need est. on … to finish with … %
"What do I need?": type a target and pick everything left or one component. It shows the average you need ("not reachable" if even 150% wouldn't do it; "already there" if 0 on the rest still gets you there). Each open item also shows what it needs on its own. A projection: never saved.
This term (term page)
A graph of each course's so far, current average and projected grade, with Need est. on the final to finish each course with … %. The target is a projection too.
Graphs
All course grades, Courses by term and Cumulative average (as a Line, or candles by Year, Semester or Class). Each graph has a Table view.
preview
In + Mark, bulk edit and delete warnings: how the course, term and cumulative averages would change. Never saved.

Settings (⚙)

UWindsor (percentage, with letter): the default; your grade is a percent with the letter beside it. UWindsor 13-point (courses before Fall 2013). Percentage → 4.0 (generic) and Percentage → 12-point (generic): common scales, not official anywhere. Custom…: opens Custom scale, one line per letter, "minimum% letter points", e.g. 85 A 4.0; points are optional but all-or-none.
Repeated courses: which attempt counts (default for every repeat)
Best attempt counts (UWindsor), Latest attempt counts (many other schools) or All attempts count.
Credits for a new course
What the wizard and + Course fill in (default 3, 0–30). Press Save.
Account… / Sign in… · Export / import… · Load demo data · Reset everything
Load demo data replaces everything (it asks first). Reset everything clears your gradebook, History and Recently deleted, but not your theme (clear this site's data in the browser for that). Neither can be undone.

Files and backups

More → Files: Excel, CSV, backup, templates (also Settings → Export / import…, and Import a file on the welcome screen). Files are made and read on your device; nothing is uploaded.

  • Backup (.json): everything, including History and Recently deleted. The one to keep.
  • Excel (.xlsx): one sheet per kind of row, with your averages beside them, and a "Read me" sheet. Keeps rules and curves, not History or Recently deleted. You can edit it and import it again: keep the Id columns.
  • CSV (marks): one row per mark (term, course, component, item). Loses rules, drop lowest, item weights, curves, excused targets, settings, History and Recently deleted.
  • Import → Choose a file… (.xlsx, .csv or .json, up to 5 MB and 20,000 rows). It shows what's in the file. Then Add to my gradebook (its terms go after yours; your settings stay) or Replace my gradebook (a backup brings its settings and History; Excel its settings; CSV uses default settings). With an empty gradebook the button just says Import. Undo in the toast right after puts the previous gradebook back.
  • Editing the Excel file: the "Read me" sheet explains it. Keep the Id columns as they are; leave Id blank for a new row. Columns marked "(calculated)" are ignored on import. An old .xls may not import: save it as .xlsx.
  • Course templates: Edit structure → Save as template downloads a course's structure with no marks. To use one: Choose a file… → pick it → Into term → Add course (you need a term first).

Accounts (optional, coming soon)

You never need an account. Signing in with Google or Microsoft only adds syncing between your devices. Sign-in is built but not switched on yet on this site: until it is, pressing Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft says "Sign-in isn’t switched on yet. Everything still works without an account." Once it's on, this is how it works:

  • Where: the person icon in the top bar, Sign in (optional) on the welcome screen, More → Sign in to sync across devices (optional), or Settings → Sign in…. Then Continue with Google, Continue with Microsoft or Not now. No password is stored here.
  • Save your gradebook to your account? lists what's stored. Press I agree or No thanks, sign out; nothing is saved to the account before you agree.
  • If your account is empty and this browser has a guest gradebook: Save to my account (a copy; the guest one stays here) or Start empty. Later: Account → Add it to my account or Replace my account’s gradebook with it (no Undo after).
  • Changes save about 2 seconds after you make them, and offline too (they're sent when you're back online). The note in the top bar says where things stand: "Saved to your account", "Saving to your account…", "Offline: saved on this device, will sync", "Not synced: changed on another device", "Signed out: changes kept on this device", "Not synced: too big for an account (see Account)" (about 1.5 MB) or "Not synced yet: will try again".
  • Changes from your other device show up when you come back to the tab ("Updated from your other device.").
  • Updated on another device — Use that version / Keep mine: see the FAQ. Signed out — Sign in again: your sign-in ended (it lasts 30 days); changes stay on this device until you sign in (Remove from this browser throws them away, for a shared computer).
  • Account: who you're signed in as and the sync state. Account + gradebook (.json) (your account and gradebook as stored on the server, for keeping, not for Import) or Excel (.xlsx), Sign out, Delete my account…, Close.
  • Sign out syncs first, then shows the guest gradebook again. If it can't sync, it asks Sign out without syncing?: Sign out anyway deletes those changes here, so download a Backup first.
  • Unsynced changes from another account (a shared browser): Download them (.json) or Discard them.
  • Details in Privacy.

FAQ

No questions match. Try fewer words, or ask me.

Where is my data stored?

Without an account: only in this browser on this device (its local storage). Nothing is sent to a server, and nobody else, including the site owner, can see it. With an account (once sign-in is switched on): in Cloudflare's database for this site, plus a copy in your browser. Only you can see it. See Privacy.

How do I back up my grades?

More → Files → Backup (.json). It holds everything (History and Recently deleted too). Keep it somewhere private. To restore: Files → Choose a file… → pick the backup → Replace my gradebook. Do this now and then: a browser can lose its data.

My marks disappeared. What happened?

Clearing your browser's data (cookies and site data), a private / incognito window, or a different browser or device all start from an empty gradebook. Grades in a private window are gone when it closes. If you have a backup, import it (Files → Choose a file… → Replace my gradebook). Deleted items, not lost data? Look in More → Recently deleted (30 days).

What's the difference between "grade so far" and "current average"?

Current average counts only what's been marked: how you're doing on graded work. So far counts every unmarked item as 0: what you'd finish with if you handed in nothing else, so it starts low and climbs. Once everything is marked they're the same, unless the weights add up to less than 100 or "drop lowest" differs between them. See the worked example.

What does "est." / projection mean?

A projection is a guess about marks you don't have yet: Projected, the Need … to finish with line, the per-item "need", the graph target and letter-only marks are labelled est. Projections are never saved as marks and never change your real averages.

How do I see what I need on the final?

Open the course. In the Need est. on … to finish with … % line, type your target (e.g. 70) and pick Final exam instead of everything left. Your other unmarked work counts at your projections (your current average where you set none). "Any mark works" or "already there" means even 0 reaches it; "over 100%" means you'd need more than full marks; "not reachable" means even 150% wouldn't do it.

How do curves work?

Two kinds. A course curve (Edit structure → Course curve): + points adds points to the course grade, × factor multiplies it, Set grade % replaces it. It applies after bonus and before Cap at 100%. An item curve (the Curve box on an item) is in that item's own points: +2, ×1.1 or =45. Curves are kept in Excel and backups, not in CSV.

I retook a course. Which grade counts?

Link the retake to the first attempt: Edit structure → Repeat of an earlier attempt?. By default the best attempt counts, as at the University of Windsor. Many schools count the latest attempt instead: change the default in Settings → "Repeated courses: which attempt counts", or for one course with Which attempt counts. Each attempt stays in its own term; only year and cumulative averages change. The link appears once you have another graded course to link to.

Why is my cumulative average higher (or lower) than every year average?

With repeats. A year average only chooses among the attempts taken that year, but the cumulative chooses among all of them, so the attempt the repeat setting picks can differ. Press the ⓘ next to Cumulative average for the details.

Which GPA scale should I use?

The one your school uses. The default, UWindsor (percentage, with letter), follows the University of Windsor's published grading policy: your grade is a percent with a letter beside it. Use UWindsor 13-point for courses before Fall 2013. The 4.0 and 12-point scales are generic, not official anywhere. For another school, use Custom… and copy its table.

My weights don't add up to 100.

That's allowed. Edit structure shows the total (Weights = 100). Over 100 counts the extra as bonus. Under 100, So far and Projected are still out of 100, but Current average is out of the weight marked. Mark real bonus parts with Bonus (on top of 100).

How does "drop lowest" work?

Set it on the component in Edit structure. It's separate for So far and Current average because in "so far" an unmarked item counts as 0 and would always be the one dropped.

I was excused from an item. Where does its weight go?

Tick Excused on the item. By default the other items in that component share its weight. To move it to another component (e.g. to the final), set Excused items go to on the component.

Do pass/fail, transfer and withdrawn courses count?

Not in averages. Passed pass/fail and transfer credits are shown separately next to your credits; withdrawn courses count nowhere. Set it with Type in Edit structure.

I pressed Ctrl+Z and nothing happened.

Click outside the text box first (inside a box, the box's own undo runs). Undo only covers changes made since you opened the page; a reload clears it. For older changes use History and Recently deleted.

Can I enter many marks at once?

Yes: Edit structure → Bulk add / edit on a component. One "Name: mark" per line (85, 85%, 17/20 or B+). A blank mark after ":" clears it. Matching names change marks, new names add items, removed lines delete nothing. + 5 numbered lines adds empty lines. You see the counts and a preview before Save, and one Undo takes it all back.

How do I copy a course to next term?

Edit structure → Duplicate course… → pick Into term. Tick Copy marks, the final grade and the curve too, or leave it to copy the structure only. Press Duplicate. In the same term the copy's title gets "(copy)". Repeat links aren't copied.

Does archiving a term remove it from my GPA?

No. Archiving only tidies the list (and + Mark skips its courses). It still counts in every average and graph.

Can I share my course setup without my marks?

Yes: Edit structure → Save as template. The file has the structure only (components, weights, items, rules), no marks, dates, notes or curves. Your classmate imports it with Files → Choose a file… → Into term → Add course (they need a term first).

How do I move my grades to another browser or computer?

Download a Backup (.json) here, then on the other device: Files → Choose a file… → Replace my gradebook. Without an account, your phone and laptop keep separate gradebooks.

Why did my rules or curves disappear after a CSV import?

A CSV holds only marks. Use the Excel file or the backup to keep rules, drops and curves.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. Downloads and imports are made and read by the page on your device.

It says "Changed in another tab — Reload".

Another tab saved first, so your last change wasn't saved (so it can't overwrite that tab's work). Press Reload, then make the change again. Keep one tab open to avoid it.

Does it work offline? Can I install it?

Yes. After one visit, the calculator and these pages open without internet, and your marks are in the browser anyway. To install it, use your browser's Install app or Add to Home Screen. It still only keeps your data on this device.

Do I need an account? When can I sign in?

No account is needed: guest mode does everything. Cloud accounts (sign in with Google or Microsoft to sync your devices) are coming soon; until they're switched on, Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft says "Sign-in isn’t switched on yet". Your guest gradebook stays in this browser either way, and you can copy it to your account later.

My school Microsoft account asks for an administrator's approval.

Some schools block new apps. Sign in with Google instead, or keep using guest mode.

I signed in with Google on one device and Microsoft on another, and my marks are missing.

They're two separate accounts, even with the same email: accounts are never joined by email. Use the same one on every device.

"Updated on another device": which do I pick?

Use that version if the other device has your newer work; Keep mine if this one does. Unsure? Download a backup first, pick one, then re-enter the few missing marks.

How do I delete everything?

Guest mode: Settings → Reset everything, then clear this site's data in your browser for the theme and view choices. With an account: Account → Delete my account… → Delete my account removes the account, its gradebook and your sign-ins from the server at once, and the account's copy from this browser. It can't be undone. Cloudflare's own recovery copies are gone within 30 days. (Reset everything while signed in empties your account's gradebook but keeps the account.)

How do I get a copy of my data?

Files → Backup (.json) (you can import it again). Signed in, also Account → Account + gradebook (.json): your account details and gradebook as stored on the server, for keeping.

What happens to my guest gradebook when I sign in or out?

It stays in this browser, untouched. After signing in you can copy it to your account. Signing out shows it again.

Why does it say "Signed out"? Does my phone work without internet?

A sign-in lasts 30 days. Press Sign in again: nothing on this device is lost. Without internet, your phone shows the last synced copy, and your changes sync when you're back online.

Can the site owner see my grades?

No. In guest mode nothing leaves your browser. With an account, the admin page shows only counts (accounts, gradebooks, storage), never anyone's grades or email.

Is this official?

No. GradeCalc is a calculator. Your school's records are what count. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Windsor.
