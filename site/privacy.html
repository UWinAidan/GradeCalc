Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-18.

GradeCalc isn't open yet. This page currently stores nothing about you and sets no cookies. The only thing kept is your light/dark theme choice, saved in your own browser.

How it will work

  • Without an account (guest mode): your grades stay in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • With an account (optional): you sign in with Google or Microsoft. GradeCalc never sees or stores a password. Before anything is saved to the cloud, you'll see a consent screen explaining what is stored.
  • What is stored for an account: your sign-in provider and its account ID, your email and name as the provider reports them, your gradebook, and when you accepted the consent screen.
  • Where: Cloudflare (Workers and the D1 database).
  • Who can see your grades: only you. The site owner's admin view shows only the number of users and total storage used, never anyone's grades.
  • Your control: download your data (JSON or Excel) or delete your account and all its data at any time from the Account page.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no third-party scripts.
