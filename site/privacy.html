Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-20.

GradeCalc works fully without an account: then everything you type stays in your own browser on this device and nothing is sent to a server. Signing in with Google or Microsoft is optional and only adds syncing between your devices. Sign-in is coming soon: it isn’t switched on yet on this site, so for now everything is guest mode.

Without an account

  • Where your grades are: in your browser's local storage for this site (localStorage), on this device only. The page never sends them anywhere. Other people, and the site owner, can't see them.
  • What else is kept there: your light/dark theme and a few view choices (your target in a course's "Need … to finish with" line and on the term graph, your recent courses for + Mark, and how the cumulative graph is shown). Also a change history (your last 1000 changes, shown per course or under History (all changes)), and Recently deleted. Deleted things are removed for good after 30 days.
  • Undo and redo (↶ ↷) are not saved. They only last while this page is open, and they start fresh when you load demo data, reset, or change something in another tab.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Reset everything deletes your terms, courses, marks, history and Recently deleted. This can't be undone. To remove everything, including your theme and view choices, clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Keep a copy: a browser can lose local storage (clearing site data, some private modes). Download a backup now and then: More → Files: Excel, CSV, backup, templates (or Settings → Export / import…).
  • Files you download or import: the Backup (.json), Excel (.xlsx) and CSV (marks) files, and course templates, are made and read by the page on your device. Nothing is uploaded. The backup holds everything, including your history and Recently deleted, so keep it somewhere private. A course template holds only the course's structure (components, weights, items, rules): no marks, dates, notes or curves.
  • Offline copy of the site: a service worker keeps a copy of this site's own pages, scripts, styles and icons in your browser's cache, so GradeCalc opens without internet and can be installed as an app. That copy holds no grades, it never stores answers from the sign-in or sync addresses, and it's refreshed each time you visit online. Clearing this site's data removes it.
  • Excel support: uses SheetJS Community Edition (Apache-2.0), served from this site, not from a third party. It only loads when you download or import an Excel file.

With an account (optional)

  • Signing in: with Google or Microsoft (a personal or a school/work Microsoft account). GradeCalc never sees or stores a password. Google or Microsoft will know you signed in to GradeCalc (their own privacy policies apply to that). Google and Microsoft are separate accounts here, even with the same email: accounts are never joined by email.
  • Consent first: signing in creates only your account details (the list below, without a gradebook) and a sign-in session. Your gradebook is not saved to your account until you press I agree on the consent screen. No thanks, sign out signs you out; if the account has no saved gradebook yet, it is deleted at once. If the consent text ever changes, you're asked again before anything more is saved.
  • What is stored for an account:
    • your gradebook (terms, courses, marks, settings, change history and Recently deleted), the same document guest mode keeps in your browser, with its size, a version number and when it was last saved. Each save replaces the last one; GradeCalc keeps no older versions;
    • which provider you used and its account ID for you (Google's account ID; Microsoft's directory and object IDs);
    • your email as the provider reports it, shown only to you (on the consent screen and your Account page) and never used to find or join accounts;
    • when the account was created, when you last signed in, and which consent text you agreed to and when;
    • sign-in sessions: a random code in a cookie (__Host-gc-session, HttpOnly, Secure, 30 days). Only a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the code is stored, never the code itself, with a second random code that protects your saves from forgery, and when the session started and ends. At most 10 sessions per account (the oldest go).
    Your name and profile picture are not stored. Your theme and view choices are never sent: they stay in this browser.
  • Cookies: only when you sign in: the session cookie above, and a short-lived one (__Host-gc-signin, 10 minutes) while you're signing in, to protect the sign-in from forgery. No other cookies, and none in guest mode.
  • Where: Cloudflare (a Worker and the D1 database for this site).
  • In this browser while signed in: a copy of your account's gradebook (kept apart from your guest gradebook) and a small note for syncing (your account number, provider, email, the version and whether there are changes not yet synced), so the page works offline. Changes made offline are sent when you're back online.
  • After you sign out: the account's copy and that note are removed from this browser (your changes are synced first; if they can't be, you're asked before they're thrown away). Your guest gradebook, theme and view choices stay. If your session ends by itself while this browser has unsynced changes, they're kept here until you sign in again, or until you press Remove from this browser in the "Signed out" banner (for a shared computer). If a different account signs in on this browser first, it's offered those changes as a download, then they're removed.
  • Who can see your grades: only you. The site owner's admin page shows only the number of accounts (per provider), the number of saved gradebooks, total storage used and the number of signed-in sessions, never anyone's grades or email.
  • Your control: Account (the person icon in the top bar) →
    • Account + gradebook (.json): your account details (provider, email, dates, consent) and your whole gradebook as stored on the server; or Excel (.xlsx);
    • Sign out;
    • Delete my account…: deletes the account, its gradebook and every sign-in session from the server right away, and the account's copy from this browser. It can't be undone.
    Your guest gradebook in this browser isn't touched by any of this. Settings → Reset everything while signed in empties your account's gradebook (on every device) but keeps the account.
  • Backups at Cloudflare: Cloudflare's database keeps its own short-term recovery history (up to 30 days), so deleted or changed data disappears from that history within 30 days. It's used only to repair the database.
  • Limits and logs: requests are rate limited per IP address and per account to stop abuse. A gradebook can be up to about 1.5 MB. Cloudflare keeps short-term request logs (for example the time, IP address and the page asked for); GradeCalc's own error log never includes your grades, email or sign-in codes.

Always

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts. The page only loads its own files.
