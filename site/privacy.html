Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-20.

Right now GradeCalc works without an account only. Everything you type stays in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and there are no cookies.

Without an account (now)

  • Where your grades are: in your browser's local storage for this site (localStorage), on this device only. The page never sends them anywhere. Other people, and the site owner, can't see them.
  • What else is kept there: your light/dark theme and a few view choices (your target in "What do I need?" and on the term graph, your recent courses for + Mark, and how the cumulative graph is shown). Also a change history (your last 1000 changes, shown per course or under History (all changes)), and Recently deleted. Deleted things are removed for good after 30 days.
  • Undo and redo (↶ ↷) are not saved. They only last while this page is open, and they start fresh when you load demo data, reset, or change something in another tab.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Reset everything deletes your terms, courses, marks, history and Recently deleted. This can't be undone. To remove everything, including your theme and view choices, clear this site's data in your browser.
  • Keep a copy: a browser can lose local storage (clearing site data, some private modes). Download a backup now and then: More → Files: Excel, CSV, backup, templates (or Settings → Export / import…).
  • Files you download or import: the Backup (.json), Excel (.xlsx) and CSV (marks) files, and course templates, are made and read by the page on your device. Nothing is uploaded. The backup holds everything, including your history and Recently deleted, so keep it somewhere private. A course template holds only the course's structure (components, weights, items, rules): no marks, dates, notes or curves.
  • Excel support: uses SheetJS Community Edition (Apache-2.0), served from this site, not from a third party. It only loads when you download or import an Excel file.

With an account (coming soon, optional)

Accounts don't exist yet. This is the plan, and this page will be updated before they go live.

  • You will sign in with Google or Microsoft. GradeCalc will never see or store a password. Before anything is saved to the cloud, you will see a consent screen explaining what is stored.
  • What will be stored for an account: your sign-in provider and its account ID, your email and name as the provider reports them, your gradebook, and when you accepted the consent screen.
  • Where: Cloudflare (Workers and the D1 database).
  • Who can see your grades: only you. The site owner's admin view will show only the number of users and total storage used, never anyone's grades.
  • Your control: you will be able to download your data (JSON or Excel) or delete your account and all its data at any time.

Always

  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no third-party scripts or fonts. The page only loads its own files.
